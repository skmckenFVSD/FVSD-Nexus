Getting started

Start here

FVSD Nexus is the division's application for moving from governed evidence to action. It brings leadership analytics, class assignments, assessment entry and annual plans into one place, and it shows you only what your role allows.

Nexus does not keep its own copy of the truth. Every number and record you see comes from a governed source:

What you see Where it comes from
Student records, assessments, annual plans Microsoft Dataverse, the division's operational record
Measures, medians and trends in analytics The FVSDAnalytics semantic model in Microsoft Fabric
Your name, role and school Your FVSD Microsoft account and your Nexus role record
Student demographics and enrolment PowerSchool, synchronized into Dataverse
Tip

If a number in Nexus looks different from a report you know, the governed source is the answer. Note what you were looking at and raise it with support (see If something does not look right).

Getting started

Sign in

Nexus uses your FVSD Microsoft account. There is no separate Nexus password.

  1. Open FVSD Nexus with the Open FVSD Nexus button at the top of this guide.
  2. If you are not already signed in, Nexus shows Use your FVSD account to continue. Select the sign-in button and sign in with your FVSD account.
  3. Nexus opens on the Overview page under Analytics.
Note

Nexus acts as you. Every record you open, save or delete is attributed to your account, and you only ever see students, schools and measures that your own permissions allow.

Getting started

Find your way around

The navigation down the left side groups everything by area. Select a group to open it, then select a page.

Group Pages Who sees it
Analytics Overview is available now. Literacy, Numeracy, Wellness, Interventions and Attendance are marked Soon. Everyone
School Administration Class Assignments and Assessments Everyone with Class Assignment access
Governance Data Quality, Data Catalog and Data Administration, all marked Soon Data Administrators only
  • Pages marked Soon are planned and cannot be opened yet.
  • Use the arrow beside the FVSD Nexus name to collapse the navigation and give the page more room. Select it again to expand it.
  • Settings sits at the bottom of the navigation, with your current role and a reminder that Nexus is secured by FVSD Entra ID.

Getting started

Your role and what you see

Your role and school assignment come from your Nexus role record. They decide which schools, classes and actions are available to you.

Role in Nexus What it usually means for you
Executive Division-wide analytics across all schools; can choose any school in filters.
School Administrator Your assigned school or schools; can delete assessment records where required.
Teacher The schools and sections you teach; class rosters and student records for your sections.
Student Support / Specialist Support Student records and history for the students you support.
Data Administrator Division-wide data access and the Governance area as it is released.

Your current role is shown at the bottom of the navigation and on the Settings page. If it is wrong, do not work around it: ask for it to be corrected.

Important

What you can see is enforced by the governed sources, not just by the screen. Hiding a button is a convenience; the data services check your permissions again every time.

Leadership analytics

Open the Overview

The Overview turns governed assessment measures into a guided picture: where results stand, where participation is low, and which school, grade and period needs attention. You do not need to know Power BI to use it.

  1. In the navigation, open Analytics and select Overview. Nexus opens here after you sign in.
  2. Set the Analysis context at the top of the page (see the next topic).
  3. Work down the page: choose a focus area, read the signals, compare schools, then focus a chart.
Note

Every value comes from the FVSDAnalytics semantic model through predefined, reviewed queries. Your own analytics permissions still apply, so two people can see different schools on the same page.

Leadership analytics

Set the analysis context

The analysis context decides which school years, periods, schools and grades every part of the page uses.

  1. Open School year and tick one or more years. Select all and Clear sit at the top of each list.
  2. Open Period and choose one or more periods.
  3. If your role allows it, open School and choose the schools to include. If you do not see School, the page already uses your own school.
  4. Under Executive Dashboard filters, open Grade and choose one or more grades.
  5. Check the Live selection line under the filters. It summarizes exactly what the page is showing.
Tip

Choosing several school years lets you compare the same period across years. In the charts, school year becomes the grouping beneath period.

Leadership analytics

Choose the focus area and assessment group

The focus area and assessment group decide which measures the signals, matrix and chart use.

  1. Under Main area of focus, select Literacy or Numeracy. Wellbeing is marked Later and is not available yet.
  2. Open Assessment group and choose the screener you want to review, for example TOSREC or TOWRE. Only assessments that apply to your selection are listed.

The page heading changes to match, for example Literacy overview.

Leadership analytics

Read “What needs our attention?”

Three short signals summarize the current selection before you look at any detail.

Signal What it tells you
Performance How median scores are moving across the selected periods.
Participation How many assessments have been submitted, and where submissions are low.
Focus Where to look first: the school, grade or period standing out from the rest.
Note

The signal wording is generated from the governed measures for your current selection. Treat the signals as a starting point for a conversation, then confirm the detail in the matrix and chart.

Leadership analytics

Compare schools in the matrix

The School comparison matrix shows the median score by period and cohort group for each school, school year and grade in your selection.

  1. Read across a row to see how one school, year and grade moved through the periods.
  2. Cohort groups (Foundation and Curriculum) stay in fixed columns so rows can be compared directly.
  3. Select a row to focus the chart beneath it on that school, year and grade.
  4. Select Clear focus above the chart, or select the same row again, to return to the full selection.

If no school matches the selected year, grade, period and assessment group, the matrix tells you so instead of showing an empty table.

Leadership analytics

Read the charts

The chart shows descriptive-term submissions and the governed median score for the selected assessment group.

  • Bars show how many submitted assessments fall into each descriptive term. The terms and their colours are the governed screener colours used everywhere in Nexus.
  • The median score line shows performance across periods.
  • Charts with no submitted assessments for your selection are not drawn.
Important

Small groups are protected. Where very few students sit behind a figure, analytics may suppress it to protect privacy. A missing value is not an error.

Students and classes

Find your class

Class Assignments is where you find a class roster and open a student. Assessment history, assessment entry and annual plans all start from here.

  1. In the navigation, open School Administration and select Class Assignments.
  2. In the Classroom Assignment Filters, check School. It is filled in from your assignment. If you work at more than one school, choose the school you need.
  3. Choose a Section Group: Literacy, Numeracy, Foundations or Homeroom.
  4. Choose a Course Name, then a Teacher. The teacher sections that match appear as you narrow the choices.
  5. Select a teacher section to load its current student roster under Assigned students.
Tip

Nexus waits until you choose a Section Group before it looks up courses and teachers. If the Course Name list is empty, check the Section Group first.

Students and classes

What each section group shows

The section group you choose decides which histories and actions open for a student.

Section group Assessment history and entry IEP / IPP annual plans
Literacy Literacy assessments Not shown here
Numeracy Numeracy assessments Not shown here
Foundations Literacy and numeracy assessments Plan history and new plans
Homeroom Not shown Plan history and new plans

If you are looking for a student's annual plan, open them from a Foundations or Homeroom section.

Students and classes

Open a student

Each student card shows the student's name, grade, date of birth, gender and SPED coding.

  1. Find the student in Assigned students, or pick them from the Student filter.
  2. Select the student card. The roster is replaced by that student's record, with the panels for your section group:
    • Assessment completion and Assessment history for Literacy, Numeracy and Foundations sections.
    • Student IEP / IPP History for Foundations and Homeroom sections.
  3. To go back, select Back to class list. The full roster returns and the history resets to Current Year.
Note

Not coded under SPED means no SPED category is recorded for the student. Grade not recorded means PowerSchool has not supplied a grade.

Students and classes

Reset or change filters

  • Changing School or Section Group clears the choices beneath it, because the courses and teachers depend on it.
  • Select Reset filters to start again from the top.
  • If No teacher sections match these filters appears, widen the Course Name or Teacher choice.

Students and classes

Reconnect Microsoft services

Sometimes your Nexus sign-in is still valid but the connection Nexus uses to read Dataverse on your behalf has expired.

  1. When you see Reconnect your Microsoft session, select Reconnect Microsoft services.
  2. Sign in again if asked. Nexus returns you to the same page.

This is expected after a long session and does not mean anything has been lost.

Recording assessments

Read a student's assessment history

Assessment history lists every saved screener result for the selected student, with the governed descriptive term and its colour.

  1. Open the student from Class Assignments in a Literacy, Numeracy or Foundations section.
  2. Under Assessment history, Current Year opens first. Select Previous Years for earlier school years.
  3. Each row shows the School year, Period, Standard score and Descriptive term.
  4. Select View on a row to open the full record.
Note

TOSREC is the first assessment connected in Nexus. Other assessments will be added in turn; until then their history is not shown here.

Recording assessments

Add a TOSREC assessment

New assessments are recorded from the student's history. Nothing is saved until you select Save Assessment.

  1. Under Assessment history, select Add assessment. Add Student Assessment opens.
  2. Choose the Assessment Type. The list is filtered by the student's curriculum and grade.
  3. Under Assessment Detail, enter the Assessment Date.
  4. Choose the Period. Only the period that matches the date is offered:
    Period Assessment dates
    Fall September 1 to December 31
    Winter January 1 to March 30
    Spring April 1 to June 30
  5. Set Exempt. If the student is exempt, enter the Exempt Reason and go to step 7.
  6. Under Assessment Scoring, choose Total Correct and Total Error. Nexus looks up and shows the Raw Score, Standard Score, Percentile Rank and Descriptive Term from the governed reference tables.
  7. Select Save Assessment. The form closes and the history refreshes with the new record.
Important

March 31, July and August do not belong to an assessment period. If No assessment period is available for this date appears, check the date.

Tip

You never type a standard score or descriptive term. They are calculated from the governed reference tables and checked again when you save, so every school scores the same way.

Recording assessments

Edit an assessment

You can correct a record while its period is still open.

  1. Select View on the record in Assessment history.
  2. Select Edit. Edit is only offered for records in the current school year and the current period.
  3. Make the change and select Save Assessment.

Records from a closed period stay read-only. If an older record is wrong, ask your school administrator; a controlled correction process is planned.

If you close the form with unsaved changes, Nexus asks Discard unsaved changes? Select Discard changes to leave without saving.

Recording assessments

Delete an assessment

Deleting is limited to School Administration and Data Administrator roles, and it cannot be undone.

  1. Select View on the record.
  2. Select Delete Assessment.
  3. Read Confirm Deletion of Record and confirm. The record is permanently removed from Dataverse and the history refreshes.
Warning

Deletion is permanent. If you are unsure, edit the record instead or ask your school administrator.

Recording assessments

Check assessment completion

The Assessments page shows which students still need an assessment in each period.

  1. In the navigation, open School Administration and select Assessments.
  2. Choose the School, Section Group, Course Name, Teacher and Assessment Type.
  3. Choose the period to review. Completion counts appear for the roster.

Which students count towards completion:

Course TOSREC required
Grades 2 to 10 Every period
ELALIT1 Winter and Spring only
ELA Not required

Exempt records count as complete.

IEP / IPP annual plans

Open a student's plan history

Each student has at most one annual plan per school year. The same plan is shown as an IEP or an IPP depending on the modules and goals it contains.

  1. In Class Assignments, choose a Foundations or Homeroom section group and open the student.
  2. Find Student IEP / IPP History. Current Year opens first; select Previous Years for earlier plans.
  3. Each row shows the Plan, School year, Grade, School and Last updated date. Select Open to open a plan.
Warning

If you see More than one plan exists for the current school year, the records need to be reconciled before another plan can be created. Contact your school administrator; do not create a workaround plan.

IEP / IPP annual plans

Create this year's annual plan

  1. In Student IEP / IPP History, select Add IEP / IPP. The button is only offered when the student has no plan for the current school year.
  2. Check the confirmation: the Student, Grade, School and Class are shown.
  3. Select Create annual plan, or Cancel to go back without creating anything.

Opening the confirmation never creates a record. Only Create annual plan does.

Every new plan starts with these modules:

  • Student Details and Screener Results, always.
  • Student Conditions and Coding, when the student is SPED coded at the time the plan is created.

IEP / IPP annual plans

Choose the plan modules

A plan contains only the modules this student needs. The selected modules appear across the top of the plan, in the division's standard order.

  1. Select Manage modules to open the module catalogue, grouped by area.
  2. Select a module to add it to the plan. Added modules show Included.
  3. Select an included module again to remove it.
  4. Select Manage modules again to close the catalogue.
  • Modules marked Locked are required and cannot be removed.
  • Selecting a module in the top navigator opens it. It does not add or remove it.
  • If someone else changes the plan's modules at the same time, Nexus reloads the plan instead of overwriting their change.

IEP / IPP annual plans

Record evidence in plan sections

Open a module from the navigator to work in it. Each module records its own evidence; you add or edit entries with the button at the top of the section and save them in a side form.

Module What you record Action
Student Details Attendance for each reporting period Update attendance
Screener Results Nothing: screener results appear automatically from assessment records View only
Oral Reading Fluency (ORF) Level of reading and words correct per minute, with the change from the previous result Add ORF result
Word Study Words Their Way starting and finishing stage and skill for each term Add Word Study record
Empower Reading Initial and current goal level and date achieved Select a goal
Mathematics / FMCS Foundations Progress against outcomes and goal levels Select an outcome
Writing Snapshot Evidence of writing development, with an optional attached writing sample Add writing snapshot
Teacher Comment and Next Steps Comments and next steps for a plan section Add comment
Student Conditions and Coding Conditions for the primary SPED category and up to three additional categories Update coding and conditions
Intervention Focus Interventions: curriculum, section, type, start date, interventionist and outcome Add intervention
Signatures Signature recipients and their routing order Add signature
Note

Attendance is entered as a whole-number percentage for each period. Leave a period blank when no value is available yet.

Tip

In Writing Snapshot, you can exclude a snapshot from the compiled document. It stays in the plan as evidence but is not printed.

IEP / IPP annual plans

Manage signatures

The Signatures module lists everyone who needs to sign or receive the plan, in routing order.

  1. Open the Signatures module and select Add signature.
  2. Choose the Recipient Type and Signatory Role, then enter the Signatory Name and Signatory Email.
  3. Set the Signatory Order. It controls the DocuSign routing order.
  4. Save the recipient. The card shows its order, Status, and the Requested and Signed dates, which are updated automatically.
Important

Enter and check parent or guardian contact details carefully. Routing order is locked after signature requests begin.

IEP / IPP annual plans

Find compiled documents

  1. In the plan, select Compiled documents to open the Annual plan files panel.
  2. Select a document and choose Download PDF.

Until the remaining plan modules are complete, Request document is not yet available and the panel may show No compiled documents yet.

Settings and support

Check your settings

Settings shows who Nexus thinks you are, which school you are assigned to, and whether the governed data sources are connected.

  1. Select Settings at the bottom of the navigation.
  2. Review each card:
Card What it shows
Governed data sources Whether Microsoft Fabric (the FVSDAnalytics semantic model) and Microsoft Dataverse are connected for you.
Assignment and role Your Current role, Primary assignment and Alternative assignment.
Named-user policy How you are signed in (FVSD Entra ID), that access is Named user, and whether the application is enabled for you.
Request a change Role, school assignment and licence change requests. This is Planned and not yet available.

If your role or school is wrong, contact support. Do not share an account or sign in as someone else to reach a school.

Settings and support

Sign out

Select Sign out on the Settings page. On a shared device, always sign out and close the browser when you finish.

Settings and support

If something does not look right

  1. Note the page, the filters or student you had selected, and what you expected to see. Do not include student names in an email or chat; describe the class and period instead.
  2. If you see Reconnect your Microsoft session, select Reconnect Microsoft services first. This fixes most loading problems.
  3. If a number differs from another report, check the Live selection line in analytics: different years, periods or grades are the most common cause.
  4. Contact support with your notes.
