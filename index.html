From Evidence to Action

How one rural Alberta division is building governed, role-aware software on Fabric, Dataverse and Power Platform, and what it taught us about trust.

Scott Mckenzie Chief Technology Officer · Fort Vermilion School Division
CDAO Canada  ·  Microsoft Power Platform & Fabric Community Events

Every organisation believes its measures are aligned. Usually that alignment is an illusion created by repetition.

The problem we set out to solve

Where trust breaks

Familiarity replaces understanding.
Recognition replaces trust.

The gap

Definition lives in one place. Logic lives in another. Interpretation lives somewhere in between.

The consequence

The number gets used long before anyone takes responsibility for it. Ownership is implied, so accountability becomes optional.

The real question

Not “is the dataset governed?” but “is the measure understood?”

The daily reality

The connective tissue between our systems is a person

A learning support teacher preparing for one meeting needs screening scores from one system, enrolment context from another, the current plan from a vendor product, and progress notes from wherever they were last written down.

  • Time. The same information re-entered and re-checked in more than one place.
  • Confidence. When two systems disagree, staff hesitate, and hesitation delays a response to a student.
  • Dependency. When a workflow lives only inside a vendor product, improvement moves at the vendor's pace and price.

The rule everything else hangs from

Three questions. Three permanent answers.

Record
Where is the record created and managed?
Dataverse

Assessments, program plans, reference data, workflow state and the audit history of who changed what, and when.

Calculation
How is a number calculated?
The semantic model

Every measure, trend and classification is defined once. No application recalculates it independently.

Identity
Who is this person, and what may they use?
Entra

Identity, licence entitlement, department and location: the organisational facts about staff.

Purview · the catalogue

Not a fourth source of truth, and that distinction is the point. Purview holds none of the record, the calculation or the identity. It publishes what they mean, who owns them and how sensitive they are, so the answer can be found and trusted rather than merely returned.

The app is where the work happens.
It is never where the truth lives.

The test every future decision has to pass

Deployed and running

Two narrow slices, built end to end

We deliberately built complete journeys rather than broad prototypes, so leadership could judge the idea on working evidence rather than a demo video.

Live

Leadership analytics

Governed semantic-model measures presented through a guided experience, not an embedded report a leader has to know how to drive. Multi-select year, period, school and grade; comparison matrix; focused charts.

Live

Class assignment & screening history

Role-scoped cascade from school to section group to course to teacher to student, then read-only screening history with governed descriptive terms and colours drawn straight from Dataverse.

Deliberately read-only. We proved identity, role scope and trustworthy reading before asking anyone to type a student result into a new system.

The non-negotiable

The app asks. It never answers.

Our applications send only predefined, reviewed queries. The browser is never handed the ability to ask the model anything it likes.

Measures

Stay defined in the semantic model. It remains the calculation authority.

Security

Row-level security and permissions follow the individual, never a pooled service account.

Surface

No general-purpose query endpoint is exposed. Ever.

The parallel build

Who owns this number, and can it be trusted?

Our Data Product Agent governs the lifecycle of every measure across Fabric, Dataverse, Power BI and Purview. Four patterns carried the weight.

  • Extract documentation, don't write it. Meaning is drawn from the model (logic, dependencies, lineage), not retyped into a document that starts drifting the moment it is saved.
  • Ownership is a gate. A measure moves forward only when a business owner, technical owner and data steward are named. Missing ownership stops registration.
  • Nothing both decides and writes. Each step hands a validated, typed contract to the next. No free-form instructions cross a boundary.
  • Exceptions route to a human. When something can't complete correctly, work stops and a named governance role reviews it. The system never guesses.
  • It publishes to Purview. Governed products land in the enterprise catalogue, so discovery arrives with ownership and sensitivity already attached.

FOIP, enforced not promised

Privacy as a property of the system

Aggregate only

No individual student rows are surfaced. Every value returned comes from a pre-defined aggregate measure.

Small groups suppressed

Where five or fewer students sit behind a figure it is suppressed. Where subtraction could still reveal it, both sides are suppressed.

Security never bypassed

The model enforces access. Users are told their scope limits a result; the system never infers what they cannot see.

Sensitivity classification travels with the record (aggregate, school, division or restricted) and governs who may receive it.

The boundary we don't trade

Canadian residency is a boundary,
not a preference

Identifiable student information, including anything student-identifiable used with AI, stays inside the approved Canadian geography.

We will not stand up a copy of student data outside Canada to gain early access to a preview tool. That has already cost us an attractive option, and it was still the right call. Evaluation happens on synthetic data or it doesn't happen.

The payoff

AI is only as trustworthy as
what it stands on

Every discipline in this deck exists so that when someone asks a question in plain language, the answer can be trusted, and so that governance survives the asking.

Ask where the work happens

Copilot, Teams and the tools staff already have, grounded in the same governed measures and catalogued meaning, not a separate copy of the data.

Permission-aware by construction

Work IQ runs every request user-scoped. It accesses only what that person is already allowed to see. The named-user principle we built on, enforced at the intelligence layer.

Already speaking the protocol

Our documentation agent is an A2A connected tool today. The governed catalogue becomes something agents can reason over, not just something people read.

Eyes open Every invocation is logged and evaluable, which is good for audit. It is consumption-billed, and residency still has to be answered before student-identifiable context goes anywhere near it.

Working today, not a mock-up

A governed measure became a plan
that goes home in a backpack

Our Foundations program plan is generated, not assembled: a Microsoft 365 Copilot skill reads the FVSD Analytics semantic model directly.

Nothing is re-keyed

Screener scores, descriptive terms and cohort placement populate straight from the governed dataset. The teacher adds professional judgement; the evidence is already there and already correct.

Built to be read by a parent

Letter portrait, proper margins, prints clean. Not a dashboard export, but a document a family reads at a kitchen table, with signatures at the end.

This is the entire argument in a single artifact: a governed measure became a document a parent can act on, and nobody copied a number to get there.

Where it runs

React and ASP.NET Core in Canada Central. Complete control over the experience, the security boundary, the region and the timing of every release.

It was the right home for a proof of concept and it remains a perfectly viable home for production. We built immediately rather than waiting for a platform to arrive.

Where it's heading

A managed backend on Fabric (database, authentication, hosting), arriving already assembled, inside the platform that already holds our analytics. For a small team, that is a staffing story before it is a technology story.

Less to own

Validation

We adopted the discipline early.
The platform is now built for it.

  • The semantic-model connection is handled by the platform. Microsoft describes that handshake as difficult to build correctly from scratch each time. That is exactly the plumbing our team writes and maintains today.
  • Access stays with the individual. Users need the same Build and Read permissions they'd need to query the model directly, so security keeps following the person.
  • Query discipline is a first-class concern, with avoiding unnecessary runtime queries cited as a reason the patterns ship in the template.

Caveat AI-assisted query generation is looser than our predefined-and-reviewed rule. We'd carry our standard forward rather than inherit the platform's.

Conditions we will not waive

Any platform move clears the same gate

  • The three sources of truth survive unchanged. First test, not last.
  • Canadian storage and processing of identifiable student information.
  • Named-user access. People continue to act as themselves.
  • Ownership and portability of what we build. We must be able to take it with us.
  • Security, auditing, accessibility and monitoring at least equal to what we have now.

Preview availability is not a strategy. We build so adoption is a cheap choice later, never a dependency now.

If you take one thing

Patterns that travel beyond education

01

Name your sources of truth, then defend them

Record, calculation, identity. Write them down. Every architecture decision becomes a test against three lines.

02

Build vertical slices, not prototypes

One narrow journey built completely earns more trust than a broad demo that works only on stage.

03

Extract documentation from the model

Anything written separately is already drifting. The model already holds the truth. Reveal it.

04

Separate deciding from writing

No component should both make a judgement and commit it. Contracts between steps, not free-form instructions.

What the discipline actually buys

The return isn't efficiency.
It's trust.

Hours saved are how you justify the invoice. Trust is why the work matters, and in public education it is the currency you cannot buy back once it is spent.

Our Board

Numbers that hold up under questioning. Every figure has a definition, an owner and a lineage; a hard question gets an answer, not a research project.

Our parents

A plan for their child that is consistent, current and defensible, because it is written from evidence rather than reassembled from four systems the night before.

Our province

Reporting we can stand behind, with privacy enforced by construction and an audit trail that already exists, because it was never bolted on afterwards.

Governance is what makes an answer defensible. Everything else in this deck is the machinery that produces one.

Governance isn't a constraint on self-service.
It's the condition that makes it possible.

Without it, the loop never closes; it just repeats the same assumptions faster
